TOKEN RIDER: THE FULL PROMPT
This is the prompt for the whole game you just beat. Paste it into Claude. Change anything in [brackets] to make your own version.

---

Build a 3D browser game called [Token Rider]. It must run from one index.html file plus three.js r149 (three.min.js next to it), with no build step, no external models and no image files: everything is drawn in code. A stranger should open the link and be playing within 2 seconds on a phone or a laptop.

STYLE
Anime cel-shaded look, like a golden-hour anime opening:
- MeshToonMaterial with a 3-step gradient map, a rim light added with onBeforeCompile, inverted-hull black outlines on characters and props.
- A gradient sky dome shader with a sun disc and glow, canvas-drawn cumulus clouds as sprites, far mountains with snow caps.
- Rolling hills built from terrain chunks that stay flat near the road, instanced grass tufts and flowers, fences along the road.
- Ambient particles per world, speed lines at high speed, blob shadows, a soft vignette.
- Fonts: Bungee for titles, Inter for UI. Glassy dark UI panels with orange accents.

PLAYER
A girl courier on a white-and-orange hoverbike: helmet with a dark visor, blue jacket, ponytail, a red scarf that flutters in two segments, twin engine flames, a cyan glow ring under the bike. Third-person camera behind and above, the bike leans when steering and tilts on jumps.

CONTROLS
Auto-forward. Steer with arrows or A/D, jump with Space/W/↑. On phones: hold the left or right half of the screen to steer, a round JUMP button and swipe up to jump.

CORE LOOP
Collect orange tokens that form curvy lines. Avoid purple bugs. 3 hearts per level (up to 5), heart pickups on the road (2 on level 1, up to 5 on level 5) and during boss fights from level 2, short invulnerability after a hit, screen shake and a red flash. Progress bar to the boss.

5 LEVELS, each a new world, one new surprise, its own boss and reward:
1. [Golden Meadow] sunset, pines, fences, pollen. New: hoppers (green bugs whose eyes flash red, then leap into your lane). Boss: RATE LIMIT GOLEM, chest says 429, throws boulders with red landing circles. Reward: a top skill.
2. [Sakura Hills] pink trees, stone lanterns, falling petals. New: low walls you must jump (an arc of tokens over each), fake tokens that flicker purple and hurt. Boss: HALLUCINATION GOLEM, crystal spikes, "???" on the chest, adds red spiked trap rings. Reward: a top skill.
3. [Copper Canyon] desert mesas, cacti, dust. New: gates with a moving gap, meteors with growing red shadows. Boss: TIMEOUT TITAN, 504, adds ground shockwaves to jump over. Reward: a top skill.
4. [Neon Night] stars, neon lamps, glowing crystals, fireflies, dark road with neon lines. New: drones sweeping side to side. Boss: NULL POINTER KNIGHT, glowing visor, moving rings. Reward: a top skill.
5. [Storm Summit] red storm sky, lightning flashes, dead trees, lava cracks, embers. Everything at once, faster. Boss: CONTEXT LIMIT TITAN, 200K on the chest, 5 HP, all attacks. Reward: this prompt.
Show a one-line hint the first time each new obstacle appears. Keep a cooldown between special obstacles so rows are always passable.

BOSS FIGHT
The boss rises from the ground with a title banner, stays 60 units ahead and attacks on a timer. Orange rings appear between you and the boss; riding through one fires a beam that removes 1 HP. Attack rate grows as HP drops. On defeat the boss breaks apart, a glowing loot crystal falls with a light pillar, then a reward card pops up.

REWARDS
Card with rarity (RARE blue, EPIC purple, LEGENDARY orange), animated glowing border, "tap to open". The modal shows the reward (for a prompt: text box, Copy button, .txt download), run stats (tokens, time, hits), Next level and Share on X buttons. Levels 1-4 unlock [the most-starred Claude skills on GitHub]: the card shows the repo name, star count, what it does and its install command, with "Open on GitHub" and "Copy install" buttons. Level 5 unlocks this prompt (stored in rewards/token-rider-prompt.txt).

MENU AND PROGRESS
Start screen: big title, "5 levels. 5 bosses. 5 rewards.", a level map with 5 nodes (locked, open, cleared), reward chips on cleared levels to reopen rewards, a RIDE button. Save progress in localStorage (wrapped in try/catch). Game over screen shows the boss error code, where you crashed and Try again / Levels buttons.

SOUND
Web Audio only: chiptune music from a 16-step scheduler with different chords and tempo per level and a faster boss theme; sound effects for tokens, hits, jumps, rings, boss hits, thunder, win fanfare. Mute button saved in localStorage.

TESTING
Add URL flags: ?level=N, ?boss, ?win, ?auto (autopilot that steers, dodges and jumps) and ?test (window.__step(n) advances the game n frames). Use them with headless Chromium to play every level, confirm each can be won and lost, and screenshot every world on desktop and phone sizes before shipping.

SHIP
Public GitHub repo with GitHub Pages from main, og:image and twitter:card summary_large_image with a 1200x630 cover screenshot, credit line "[Built with Claude + three.js · by @you]".
